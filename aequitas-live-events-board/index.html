Our Mission

Equality is more than an idea.

Aequitas is here to turn fairness into action across the Alabama School of Healthcare Sciences.

We believe a strong school gives every student a meaningful chance to contribute. That means paying attention to who gets opportunities, whose voice is heard, and who may be left out.

Our goal is not to stand above the ASHS community. It is to work within it and help make the opportunities of this school more equal for everyone.

◆Listen. Make room for student voices.
◆Act. Turn concerns into practical solutions.
◆Include. Help opportunities reach the whole school.
“Aequitas does not just believe in equal opportunity. We work to help create it.”
The Aequitas Standard

Three qualities. One purpose.

The principles that guide how our house represents ASHS.

01

Justice

We stand up when something is unfair and believe students deserve respect, consistency, and a voice.

02

Peace

We value understanding, respectful disagreement, and a school environment where people can work together.

03

Equality

We work toward equal opportunities so students can participate, lead, and contribute without being overlooked.

Our Responsibility

Making opportunity available to everyone.

Aequitas has a unique role at ASHS: helping the school recognize when opportunities are uneven and encouraging solutions that make participation more accessible.

We want to champion ideas that strengthen the entire school—not just our house.

The Aequitas Constitution

What we stand for.

Our principles are meant to guide decisions, not decorate a poster.

Aequitas will advocate for fair treatment and will speak up when students are overlooked or treated inconsistently.
Aequitas will promote respectful communication and cooperation, even when students disagree.
Aequitas will support equal access to meaningful school opportunities and encourage participation from across the ASHS community.
Aequitas will not treat leadership as a title. Ideas should become projects, conversations, and measurable improvements.
